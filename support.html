Common questions

How do I use Bridge on another phone?

Go to Profile → Settings → Save your account and add your email. Then on the other phone tap “I Have an Account” and enter the code we email you. No password needed.

I lost my guest account

Guest accounts live only on the device where you started. If you never saved it with an email, we can't restore it — sorry. Save your account to avoid this.

How do I report or block someone?

Open their profile and tap Report or Block. You can also report a chat message (tap “Report” under it) or an item in someone's library. Reports are private.

Who can see my library?

You choose in Profile → Settings → Who can see my library: Everyone, Friends only (default), or Only me.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete account, then type DELETE to confirm. This permanently deletes your account, profile, library, friends, answers and chat messages. Bridges you shared stay for the other person, shown as “Deleted user”. You can also email us from your account email to ask us to delete it.
