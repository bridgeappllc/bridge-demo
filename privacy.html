Privacy Policy

Bridge LLC · Effective date: [to be set when published] · Last updated: October 11, 2026

Bridge is an app for listening to the same thing as a friend, answering three questions, and talking about where you see it differently. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising.

Who we are

Bridge is operated by Bridge LLC, Hawaii, USA. Questions or requests: bridgeappllc@gmail.com.

We don't collect your contacts, location, photos, microphone, or advertising identifiers. Bridge has no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking cookies. The app stores your sign-in session and a few preferences in your device's local storage so you stay signed in.

Who can see your information

  • Your bridge partner sees your name, your answers (only after they've submitted their own), and the chat on that bridge.
  • People signed in to Bridge can find your name, @username and bio in search and on your profile, unless you've blocked each other.
  • Your library is visible to Everyone, Friends only (default — your friends plus people you've been on a bridge with), or Only me — you choose in Settings.
  • Bridge LLC can access data to operate the service, respond to reports, and comply with law. We don't read your chats except when needed to review a report, keep the service secure, or when required by law.

Service providers

We use these companies to run Bridge. They process data on our behalf, only for that purpose:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and data hosting (servers in the United States, us-west-1).
  • Resend — delivers the sign-in code emails (receives your email address and the email content).
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the Bridge web app files (receives your IP address when the app loads).
  • Google Fonts — provides the app's fonts (Google receives your IP address when fonts load).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — if you install the mobile app, they handle downloads under their own privacy policies.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with third parties for their own marketing or for targeted advertising. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of Bridge LLC (with notice to you).

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete your account, profile, email address, library, friendships, blocks, answers and chat messages right away. Bridges you shared with someone stay for that person, shown as "Deleted user", without your answers or messages. Reports are kept for up to 12 months for safety (the reporter is removed if they delete their account). Copies may remain in our providers' backups and logs for a limited period before they are overwritten.

Guest accounts that haven't been saved with an email can't be recovered once you sign out or lose your device.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name, topics, @username, bio and library visibility any time in Profile → Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. Or email bridgeappllc@gmail.com from your account email and we'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Get a copy of your data or ask a question: email us. Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to or restrict processing. We'll honour these requests and won't discriminate against you for making them.

Children

Bridge is not for children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to use it. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using Bridge, email us and we'll delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules in our database so people only see what they're allowed to. No system is perfectly secure; please use an email account you control.

Changes

If we make material changes, we'll update this page and let you know in the app before they take effect.
