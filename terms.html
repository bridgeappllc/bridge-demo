Tips

  • Tips for listening. You can add a tip for someone in your pod to listen to a clip. You pay when you add it. We hold it until they answer the clip's takeaway question (and rate it), and then it's theirs.
  • Refunds if they don't answer. If they haven't answered within 30 days, or the clip or pod is deleted, or they leave the pod, the tip is refunded to you automatically.
  • Thank-you tips. You can also tip someone as a thank-you for a clip they shared or from their profile. These are voluntary gifts: you get nothing in return, and nobody has to tip.
  • Earn first, then get paid. Anyone can be tipped, even before setting up payouts. Once you've earned a tip, Settings shows "Get paid", and you set up a Stripe account to receive it. Tips not claimed within 90 days of being earned are refunded to the tipper.
  • Pod Club keeps 10%. The person you tip receives 90% of the tip amount, and Pod Club keeps 10%.
  • You pay processing. A processing fee is added on top of the tip at checkout and shown before you pay, so the full 90% reaches the recipient.

  • Stripe. Payments are processed by Stripe. People who receive tips agree to Stripe's terms and are responsible for any taxes on what they receive. We may pause tipping or an account to prevent fraud or abuse.
